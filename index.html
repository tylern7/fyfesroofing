Roofing 101

Good questions, straight answers.

What should I expect during a roof inspection and repair?

We start with a consultation to confirm your roof’s symptoms and scope, then do a thorough on-site inspection. You get a detailed written proposal covering the work, materials and timeline. Our crew carries out the job and we do a final inspection before we call it done.

When is the best time to book a roof inspection?

Late summer or early fall, so damaged shingles, failing flashing and ventilation problems are found before winter freeze-thaw cycles and ice dams. If you see leaks after storms, missing shingles or granule loss, or you have had heavy wind, hail or snow, book sooner.

Do you handle emergency leaks?

Yes. We offer 24-hour emergency repair for leaks, missing shingles and storm damage, to limit interior water damage, mould and structural problems. Call (905) 242-2820.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

We document the damage and provide a detailed estimate you can submit to your insurer, then coordinate repairs once the claim is approved, so the roof scope and the claim line up.

What warranty do you offer?

Every project comes with a 10-year workmanship warranty and a written contract.

How can I pay?

We accept various payment methods, including cash and cheque.
